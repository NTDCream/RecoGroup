## Section Guidelines

### Executive Summary
- 2-3 paragraphs max
- Standalone - reader should get main points
- No citations needed
- Write last, after content

### Detailed Sections
- Organize by logical themes
- Use subsections for depth
- Include inline citations
- Each learning should appear somewhere

### Key Insights
- 5-7 bullet points
- Actionable and memorable
- Derived from analysis, not just facts

### Conclusions
- Synthesize, don't repeat
- Make recommendations if applicable
- Acknowledge limitations
